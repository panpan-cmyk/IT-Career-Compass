Which corner of IT is actually built for you?

You don't need any prior tech experience for this. It's not a knowledge test — it's a compass. Eight short reasoning questions and twenty-eight quick interest statements map how you think and what you enjoy against seven beginner-friendly IT fields, so you can start in the one you're most likely to enjoy and be good at.

How to answer:

The first 8 questions are short reasoning puzzles — they have a correct answer, but you don't need any IT background to work them out. Take your best guess if you're unsure. The remaining 28 are about interests and working style — there are no right or wrong answers there, so go with what's honestly true for you, not what sounds impressive.

For educators: this is a self-assessment and discussion-starter for students with no IT background yet, not a certified psychometric instrument. Use the results to open a conversation about strengths and first courses, not to gate students out of any path.
